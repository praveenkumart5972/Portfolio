about

Electronics & Communication Engineering student, final stretch of a four-year degree, with a focus that narrowed early to embedded systems and firmware. Most of my time goes into Embedded C, bare-metal register programming, and STM32 — the layer where code meets hardware directly.

I care about writing firmware that's reliable and easy to reason about: clean ISRs, predictable timing, no guesswork. Currently deepening my grip on FreeRTOS — tasks, queues, semaphores — and on communication protocols like UART, SPI, and I2C.

The goal for the next six months: turn this into an entry-level embedded engineering role in Japan. I'm studying Japanese in parallel — currently at JLPT N4 — because the language is part of the job, not an afterthought.
